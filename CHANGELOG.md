# Changelog

## 1.8.1 (24) — 2026-09-08

Initial public source snapshot, following earlier local iterations.

- Native SwiftUI settings for six Codex Micro controls, with English and Simplified Chinese.
- Shortcut recording, single-key selection and app activation/hiding with optional follow-up shortcuts.
- Lightweight permission guidance, consistent app icons and refined key-layout spacing.
- Exact-match ACT12 placeholder checks and merged ACT10/ACT11 handling.
- Local mapping storage, bounded in-memory diagnostics and no telemetry implementation.
- Public build/install scripts, CI, bilingual documentation and source-based privacy details.

The initial binary targets Apple silicon and is ad-hoc signed, without Apple notarization. Automated tests do not substitute for physical-device and macOS permission validation.

首次公开源码快照，基于此前的本地迭代。包含原生中英文设置界面、快捷键录制和选键、应用控制、轻量权限引导、ACT12 精确验证、双开关合并，以及构建脚本、CI 和隐私说明。首个二进制面向 Apple Silicon，使用本地签名，未经过 Apple 公证；自动测试不能代替真实设备和系统权限验证。
